Signs you may have storm damage

  • Dented gutters, vents or AC units
  • Missing, cracked or lifted shingles
  • Granules collecting in gutters
  • Water stains on ceilings

Why act fast?

Most insurance policies have time limits for filing storm claims, and small openings let water into your decking and attic. An early inspection protects both your home and your claim.
