What we look for on your roof

Real photos from Bunton Roofing inspections around Abilene. Damage like this is easy to miss from the ground.

Wind damage

Lifted, creased or missing shingles that let water reach your decking.

Hail & granule loss

Bruised shingles and bare spots that age your roof years early.

Call us if you notice

  • Dented gutters, vents or AC units
  • Shingle granules in your gutters
  • Missing or lifted shingles after wind
  • Water stains on ceilings or in the attic
  • Neighbors getting new roofs after a storm
